# Your first task

This walkthrough assumes a task has been created and its workflow has reached a step assigned to you. If no work is assigned, ask a colleague or see [Creating work](../creating-work.md).

## Find and open your work

1. Sign in and open **My Work**. Find your task in the inbox; use the search and filters if the list is long.
2. Open the task. Review its description, project, files, activity, and current step as available.
3. If you can only watch the task, another person may have claimed the step or you may not be the assignee. Ask your workspace administrator if you need access.

## Complete a step

Fill in required fields, select the available decision if the step asks for one, and submit. Check any validation message and correct the fields if submission fails. A successful submission records your decision and advances the workflow; the task may remain active if more steps are required. A later step can be routed to another person or wait for an assignment.

Return to **My Work** to check your remaining work, or use the task's activity and run views to see progress. See [Task detail](../task-detail.md) and [My Work](../my-work.md) for more information.
