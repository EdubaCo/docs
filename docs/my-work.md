# My Work / Task inbox

**My Work** lists open tasks available to you. Workflow steps can be assigned to an individual, role, or group; the applicable rules determine who can act. A task held by someone else may appear as watch-only.

## Find and prioritize work

Search the list, filter by workflow, SLA or context, and group by project, workflow or deadline week. Sort by SLA priority or due date. You can save a filtered view for quick access later. Rows show the task, workflow, context, deadline, current step and assignees where available.

Open a task to inspect its details and submit your action. See [Task detail](./task-detail.md). For shared work, available actions can include **Claim** to take a step and **Release** to return it to the pool. A watch-only task can be viewed but cannot be submitted by you while held by someone else.

## Arrange cover

Under **Settings → Away & delegation**, you can delegate your own roles to a colleague for a selected period. The delegate receives the permissions granted by those roles during that period. Administrators need `manageDelegations` to manage delegations for other members. See [Settings](./settings.md).

If an expected task is missing, clear the filters and check whether the workflow has reached your step. Ask an administrator to check assignment and access if needed.
