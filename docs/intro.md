# Eduba documentation

Welcome to the Eduba platform documentation. This site is the product reference for the platform, available in English and Persian.

**New here? Start with [Getting started](./getting-started.md).** It explains
what Eduba is, the handful of core ideas everything else builds on, and walks you
through completing [your first task](./getting-started/your-first-task.md).

## How this documentation is organized

The documentation follows the product's information architecture. Each area maps to part of the app:

- **[Getting started](./getting-started.md)** — orientation,
  [sign-in and workspace access](./sign-in-and-access.md), and
  [key concepts](./getting-started/key-concepts.md).
- **[My Work](./my-work.md)** and **[Task detail](./task-detail.md)** — your
  task inbox and working a task.
- **[Run inspector](./run-inspector.md)** — inspecting workflow runs, nodes,
  timelines, and escalations.
- **[Global search](./search.md)** — finding tasks, projects, documents, and
  workflows with the command palette.
- **[Creating work](./creating-work.md)** — starting runs and tasks.
- **[Projects](./projects.md)** and **[Documents](./documents.md)** — grouping
  related work, managing versioned files, and applying customer revisions.
- **[Workflow designer](./workflow-designer.md)** and
  **[Subflow library](./subflow-library.md)** — defining how work flows.
- **[Policy rules](./policy.md)**, **[Roles and org chart](./roles-org.md)**,
  **[User groups](./groups.md)**, **[Delegations](./delegations.md)**,
  **[Procedures & standards library](./standards-library.md)**, and
  **[Pack management](./packs.md)** — administering access, standards, and
  reusable packs.
- **[Notifications](./notifications.md)**, **[Inbound email](./inbound-email.md)**,
  **[Chat](./chat.md)**, and **[AI assistant](./ai.md)** — alerts, email
  ingestion, collaboration, and assistance.
- **[Settings](./settings.md)**, **[Security & audit](./security.md)**,
  **[Storage & connectors](./storage-and-connectors.md)**,
  **[Plan & referrals](./subscription-and-referrals.md)**, and
  **[Workspace articles](./workspace-articles.md)** — workspace configuration,
  security, infrastructure connectors, subscription, internal knowledge base,
  and personal preferences.
- **[App admin](./app-admin.md)** — operating the deployment itself.

:::note Documentation and access
The documentation site serves pages publicly. In-app help filters its links according to your capabilities; seeing a page does not grant the permissions required to use the feature.
:::
