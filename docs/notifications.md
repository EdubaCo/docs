# Notifications

Eduba notifies you as work moves through your workspace — when a step lands in
your inbox, an SLA deadline approaches, a run fails, a referenced standard
updates, or a customer revision resets a task. Notifications appear in real time
in the top-bar bell and can also be delivered by email according to your
preferences.

## The notification bell and feed

- Select the **bell icon** in the top bar to see your latest notifications and
  your unread count.
- Select **View all** to open the full **Notifications** screen.
- Switch between **All** and **Unread** to narrow the feed.
- Select a notification to jump straight to the related task, run, project, or
  library item, or mark individual items or **Mark all read**.

## Events that generate notifications

| Event | When you receive it |
| --- | --- |
| **Task assigned** | A task or workflow step is assigned to you or one of your roles or groups. |
| **SLA warning** | A step you hold is approaching its SLA deadline. |
| **Review requested** | A step requests your review or decision. |
| **Workflow completed / failed** | A workflow run you participate in finishes or fails. |
| **Standard new version** | A newer version is detected for a procedure or standard you watch or reference. |
| **Step has no assignee** | A workflow step cannot be routed to any active user and needs administrator attention. |
| **Workflow step failed / escalated** | A step fails automatically, or a blocked participant escalates a failed step to administrators. |
| **Customer revision applied** | A project's customer revision reset supersedes an input used by your task. |

## Delivery preferences

Open **Settings → Notifications** to choose how each event type reaches you:

- **In-app only** — shows in the bell and notifications feed without sending an
  email.
- **In-app and email** — shows in the app and also sends an email (when
  workspace email delivery is configured).
- **Muted** — suppresses notifications for that event type.

Each change saves automatically. If emails do not arrive even when **In-app
and email** is selected, ask a workspace administrator to verify outbound email
(SMTP) configuration.
